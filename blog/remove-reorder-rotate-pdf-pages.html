Remove, Reorder and Rotate PDF Files: A Quick Guide

Guide4 min read2026-06-09

Fix a messy PDF: delete pages you do not need, reorder PDF pages and rotate PDF files that came out sideways.

Quick summary

  • Three small fixes make a PDF look professional: remove, reorder, rotate.
  • Do them in that order, then add page numbers as a last step.
  • Every fix creates a new file and leaves your original untouched.

Before you send a PDF, take two minutes to tidy it. These three fixes cover most problems.

1. Remove pages you do not need

Cover sheets, blank pages and drafts do not help anyone. Use Remove Pages, type the numbers like 2, 5-7 and you get a new file without them. The pages are really deleted, not just hidden. For empty back sides of a double-sided scan, Delete Blank Pages finds them automatically.

2. Put pages in the right order

Scanned the pages in the wrong order? Reorder Pages shows the current order. Change the numbers around, for example 3, 1, 2, and the file is rebuilt in that order. If everything is simply backwards, Reverse Pages does it in one click.

3. Fix sideways pages

Scanners and phone apps often save pages sideways. Rotate PDF turns them and saves the fix, so the file looks right for everybody, not just on your screen. You can rotate all pages or only the ones you list.

A good order of work

  1. Remove the pages you do not want.
  2. Reorder what is left.
  3. Rotate pages that are sideways.
  4. Add numbers with Add Page Numbers, so everyone can refer to page 4 and mean the same thing.

When you need only a few pages

If you want to keep just a handful of pages, it is quicker to use Extract Pages than to delete everything else.

Keep it safe

All these tools run in your browser. Your document is not uploaded, and you always download a new file instead of overwriting the original.

Frequently asked questions

Can I remove and reorder pages at the same time?

Not in one step. Remove pages first, then reorder the new file.

Does rotating a page lower its quality?

No. Only the orientation of the page changes.

Will page numbers update after I remove pages?

Existing printed numbers will not change. Add page numbers as the last step so they are correct.

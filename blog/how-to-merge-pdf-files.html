How to Merge PDF Files Into One

How-to4 min read2026-02-10

Step-by-step guide to merge PDF files into a single document in the right order. Free, no install, no signup and your files stay private.

Quick summary

  • One clean PDF is easier to send, print and keep than five separate attachments.
  • The first file in your list becomes the first pages of the merged document.
  • Fix order, then compress and number pages after merging, not before.

Sending five separate attachments is annoying for you and for the person who receives them. One tidy PDF is easier to read, easier to print and harder to lose. With Merge PDF you can join documents in under a minute, without installing anything.

When merging PDFs helps

  • Job applications: CV, cover letter and certificates in one file.
  • Invoices or receipts combined for an expense claim.
  • Scanned pages that came out as separate files.
  • Chapters or reports written by different people.
  • Visa, school or bank applications that accept only a single upload.

How to merge PDF files

  1. Open the Merge PDF tool and add all your files. You can drag them in or click the box.
  2. Use the arrow buttons to put them in the order you want.
  3. Press Merge PDFs.
  4. Download your combined document.

Merging on a phone

The same tool works in a phone browser. Tap the upload box, pick the files from your storage and use the arrows to order them. If your documents are photos rather than PDFs, convert them first with JPG to PDF, then merge the results.

Things to watch out for

Password-protected files cannot be merged until the password is removed. Mixed page sizes are fine, but they look different when printed. If the final file is too large to email, run it through Compress PDF afterwards.

After you merge

A combined document often needs a little cleanup. Remove unwanted pages with Remove Pages, fix the order with Reorder Pages and finish with Add Page Numbers, so everyone can refer to the same page.

Is it private?

Yes. Merging happens inside your browser. Your documents are not uploaded to a server, which matters for contracts, ID scans and financial papers.

Frequently asked questions

How do I merge PDF files for free?

Open the Merge PDF tool, add your files, set the order and press Merge PDFs. It is free and needs no account.

Can I merge PDFs in a different order?

Yes. Use the up and down arrows next to each file to change the order before merging.

Is there a limit to how many PDFs I can combine?

We do not set a limit. Very large batches depend on your device memory.

How to Split a PDF Into Separate Pages or Files

How-to4 min read2026-07-08

Learn how to split a PDF into single pages or custom page ranges. Free, fast and private, with tips for sending only the pages you need.

Quick summary

  • Split PDF can give you one file per page or one file per range you type.
  • Pages are copied, not re-compressed, so quality stays the same.
  • If you want just one new file with chosen pages, Extract Pages is quicker.

A single large PDF is not always what you need. Sometimes only chapter three matters, or the one page with a signature. Split PDF cuts a document exactly where you tell it to.

Two ways to split

Every page as its own PDF. Good for scanned bundles, such as a stack of invoices that was saved as one file. You get a ZIP with one PDF per page.

Custom ranges. Type the ranges separated by commas, such as 1-3, 4-6, 7. Each part becomes its own file, so that example gives you three PDFs.

Step by step

  1. Open Split PDF and add your document.
  2. Choose Every page or Custom ranges.
  3. If you chose ranges, type them in the box.
  4. Press Split PDF and download your files. Several files arrive in one ZIP.

Split or extract?

Splitting gives you several files. If you only want one new file with a few chosen pages, such as pages 4 to 9 of a long report, use Extract Pages instead. It saves you from unzipping and picking.

Why people split PDFs

  • Sharing only the relevant chapter or section.
  • Getting under an upload limit by sending a document in parts.
  • Separating scanned documents that were saved together.
  • Keeping private pages out of a file you share.

After splitting

If any part is still too large, use Compress PDF. If you later need to join pieces again, Merge PDF puts them back together in the order you choose.

Privacy and quality

Splitting runs in your browser and nothing is uploaded. Because pages are copied as they are, fonts, images and layout stay sharp.

Frequently asked questions

How do I split a PDF into individual pages?

Choose Every page becomes its own PDF in Split PDF. The result is a ZIP with one file per page.

How do I split a PDF by chapter?

Look up the page range of each chapter and enter them as 1-12, 13-28, 29-40. Each range becomes a separate PDF.

Will splitting reduce the quality?

No. Pages are copied without re-compression.

    About JPG to PDF

    JPG to PDF turns one photo or a whole batch of photos into a single PDF. Each picture becomes one page, you can reorder them, and you choose between a neat A4 sheet or a page that matches the photo exactly.

    Perfect for ID cards, receipts, homework pages and anything you photographed on your phone. Each image becomes one page. Photos taken in portrait stay upright because we read the camera's rotation info.

    How to use JPG to PDF

    1. Add your photos. Drag in as many as you like.
    2. Put them in order with the arrows, and pick a page size.
    3. Press Create PDF and download your file.

    When to use JPG to PDF

    • Send ID cards, receipts or certificates as one tidy attachment.
    • Turn photographed homework or notes into a document your teacher can open anywhere.
    • Build a photo portfolio or a portrait album as a printable PDF.
    • Submit documents to a portal that accepts PDF but not images.

    Tips for better results

    • Photos from phones are read with their camera rotation, so portrait pictures stay upright.
    • Pick A4 for printing and Same size as the image when you want no borders at all.
    • If the PDF becomes too large for email, run it through Compress PDF with the Strong level.

    Why use 40PDF for this?

    Your photos are never sent to a server. The PDF is assembled on your device, which is exactly what you want for passports, bank cards and other private pictures.

    JPG to PDF: questions and answers

    Can I add more than one photo?

    Yes. Each image becomes one page, in the order shown in the list.

    Will my photos be compressed?

    They are saved at high JPEG quality. If the file is too big, run it through Compress PDF afterwards.

    Does it work on my phone?

    Yes. Tap the upload box and choose photos from your gallery or camera.

    How do I convert a JPG to PDF?

    Open JPG to PDF, add your image, choose the page size and margin and press Create PDF. Download the PDF when it is ready.

    Can I convert several JPG files into one PDF?

    Yes. Add as many photos as you like, use the arrows to set the order and they will all go into one multi-page PDF.

    Is it safe to convert personal photos here?

    Yes. The conversion runs inside your browser and the pictures are not uploaded.
